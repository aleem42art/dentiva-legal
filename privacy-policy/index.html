Dentiva is a dental clinic management app published by Cavitex (“Cavitex”, “we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the app handles, where it is kept, when it leaves your device, and the choices you have. It applies to the Dentiva app on Android and on desktop.

At a glance

  • Your clinic's records stay on your device. Cavitex does not run servers that receive them, and we cannot see your patients' information.
  • No advertising, analytics or tracking. The app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • Cloud backup is optional and encrypted. If you turn on Google Drive backup, each backup is encrypted on your device before upload and stored in a private app folder in your own Google Drive.
  • Information is shared only when you choose. For example, when you send an invoice over WhatsApp or save a PDF.
  • We never sell data.

1.Who we are

Dentiva is developed and published by Cavitex. If you have any question about this policy or about how Dentiva handles information, contact us at cavitexdental@gmail.com.

2.Your clinic's role and ours

Dentiva is a tool that dental clinics use to keep their own records. The clinic (the dentist or practice using the app) decides what patient information to record, why, and for how long. The clinic is responsible for that information and for following the health-record, privacy and professional rules that apply to it, including getting any patient consent those rules require.

Cavitex provides the software. Dentiva works offline and stores records on the clinic's own devices, so Cavitex does not receive, host or process patient records, and we have no technical means to view them.

3.Information the app handles

Clinic staff enter this information, or the app creates it as it is used. Unless section 7 says otherwise, it stays on the device.

3.1 Clinic and staff accounts

Clinic name, address and registration details (such as a PMDC number); each staff member's name, login ID and role (for example, doctor or assistant); an optional profile photo; optional details such as age, gender and nationality; and passwords and security-question answers. Passwords and answers are never stored in readable form. They are salted, hashed with PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 and kept in the operating system's protected storage.

3.2 Patient information, including health information

Identity and contact details (name, age, gender, phone number, email and address); medical history, allergies, drug allergies, blood group and medical notes; dental charts, diagnoses, treatment plans, procedures, prescriptions, clinical notes and laboratory cases; and appointments and visit history. This is sensitive health information and must be treated as confidential.

3.3 Billing and payment records

Fees, invoices, payments, instalments and outstanding balances. Dentiva records payments the clinic receives. It does not process card payments or collect bank or card details.

3.4 Photos, radiographs and documents

Clinical photos taken with the camera, and images or files the clinic selects on the device, such as radiographs. The app also creates PDF documents such as invoices, treatment reports and clinic summaries.

3.5 Google account information (only if you use Google Drive backup)

When you connect a Google account for backup, Google Sign-In gives Dentiva the account's email address, name and profile picture. Dentiva uses them only to show which account is connected, and stores the email address on the device for that purpose. See section 8.

3.6 Technical information

A random identifier created when the app is first installed, used to keep invoice numbers and records from different devices apart; and the app's settings and preferences. These stay on the device and inside its backups.

3.7 Information we do not collect

Dentiva does not collect location, contacts, call logs, SMS messages or browsing activity, and does not use the microphone. It does not include advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services. Fingerprint and face data never reach the app: the device checks them and tells Dentiva only whether the check succeeded.

4.How information is used

The app uses this information only to provide its features to the clinic: keeping patient and treatment records; scheduling appointments and reminders; producing invoices, prescriptions and reports; signing staff in and applying their roles; and making and restoring backups.

Cavitex does not use clinic or patient information for advertising, profiling or any purpose of its own, and does not sell it.

5.Where information is stored

Records are kept in a database in Dentiva's private storage on the device. Android keeps this storage separate from other apps, and the device's own storage encryption protects it while the device is locked. Dentiva does not add a separate encryption layer to this database.

Android's automatic cloud backup of app data is turned off for Dentiva, so the operating system does not copy the database to Google.

We strongly recommend protecting every clinic device with a screen lock and keeping it up to date.

6.Backups

6.1 Backups on the device

Dentiva can back up the clinic's data automatically and on request. Backups are saved in the app's private storage, or in a folder you choose with Android's folder picker. Dentiva gets access only to the folder you choose, not to your other files. Automatic backups are trimmed to the newest 10, plus the newest one from each of the last 7 days. Manual exports, and the safety copy made before a restore, are kept until you delete them.

Dentiva does not encrypt local backup files and exports. They contain full patient records, so store and share them as carefully as the records themselves. Backups in the app's private storage are removed when the app is uninstalled; backups in a folder you chose stay there.

6.2 Google Drive backup (optional)

Drive backup is off until you turn it on. Setup asks whether the clinic should stay on this device only or also back up to Google Drive, and explains what that means before Dentiva contacts Google at all. If you keep it off, the app never signs in to Google. You can change the answer at any time in Backup & Data, and disconnecting stops Dentiva contacting Google on that device.

If you connect a Google account, Dentiva can also upload backups to Google Drive.

  • Encrypted before upload. Each backup is compressed and encrypted on your device with AES-256-GCM. The encryption key is not uploaded, and neither Cavitex nor Google can read the contents.
  • Private app folder. Backups are stored in the hidden app-data folder of your own Google Drive. Dentiva asks only for access to that folder (the drive.appdata permission), so it cannot see or change any other file in your Drive, and other apps cannot see Dentiva's backups.
  • Limited label information. Each backup file carries some unencrypted details needed to list and restore it: when it was made, the app and database versions, and how many patient records and attachments it contains. No names or clinical details are included.
  • Five backups kept. Dentiva keeps the five most recent Drive backups and deletes older ones automatically.
  • You stay in control. You can disconnect the account, or delete all Dentiva backups from Drive in the app, at any time.

6.3 Restoring a Drive backup

The key that encrypts Drive backups is created on the device and never leaves it. A Drive backup can therefore be restored only on the device that made it, and only while Dentiva stays installed on that device. It is not a way to move a clinic to a new phone or to recover from a lost device.

For those, use the app's own backup file: export it from Backup & Data and keep it somewhere safe. A portable recovery key for Drive backups is planned; this page will say so when it ships.

7.When information leaves the device

Information leaves the device only in the cases below, and each one is started by a person using the app.

  • Google Drive backup: encrypted, as described in section 6.2.
  • Sending documents and messages: when you send an invoice, a treatment report or a message, Dentiva passes it to the app you choose (for example WhatsApp, email or a printer) through the device's share or print feature. You choose the recipient and confirm the send in that app. After that, the information is handled by that app and the recipient under their own terms.
  • Exporting backups: to a location you choose, as described in section 6.1.
  • Contacting us: if you email us, including from the in-app Feedback screen, we receive your email address and what you write. The Feedback screen can add basic diagnostics (app version, platform and database version). You can see them before sending and switch them off. Please do not include patient information in support emails.

Cavitex does not sell, rent or share information with advertisers or data brokers. We would disclose information we hold, such as a support email, only if the law requires it.

8.Google user data

Dentiva's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Dentiva uses Google Sign-In and the Google Drive API only to store, list, download and delete the clinic's own encrypted backups in the app-data folder of the connected account. This data is not transferred to Cavitex or anyone else, is not used for advertising, and is not read by people.

You can remove Dentiva's access at any time from your Google Account's Third-party apps & services page. You can delete Dentiva's hidden backup data in Google Drive under Settings → Manage apps.

10.Security

Dentiva is designed to protect the records it holds:

  • Passwords and security answers are salted and hashed, and kept in the operating system's protected storage.
  • Repeated failed sign-ins bring increasing waits and temporary lockouts.
  • Staff roles limit what each person can do.
  • Optional biometric unlock is tied to the account that turned it on.
  • Google Drive backups are encrypted on the device and sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.
  • Backups are checked for integrity before they are restored.
  • The app is designed not to write patient information or passwords to its logs.

No system is perfectly secure. How well records on a device are protected also depends on the clinic: use a screen lock, keep devices updated, give each staff member their own account, and keep exported backup files safe.

11.Retention and deletion

Records stay on the device until the clinic deletes them. Dentiva provides these controls:

  • Deleting a patient removes the patient and their records from every screen, search and report. So that deletions stay consistent across backups and devices, the deleted entries are kept on the device marked as deleted, and can appear inside backup files made afterwards. A factory reset erases them permanently.
  • Removing a staff member deletes their sign-in credentials from the device.
  • Factory reset (in Settings) permanently erases all Dentiva data on the device: records, staff accounts, credentials, settings, photos and files.
  • Uninstalling Dentiva removes its private data from the device. Backups saved to a folder you chose, and backups in Google Drive, are not removed by uninstalling.
  • Google Drive backups can be deleted in the app, or in Google Drive under Settings → Manage apps.

Dentiva has no online accounts. Staff accounts exist only on the clinic's devices, so there is no online account for us to delete; everything is removed with the controls above.

Emails you send us are kept for as long as we need them to help you and to keep a record of support history. We delete them on request.

12.Your rights and choices

Patients

The clinic that treats you controls your records. To see, correct, get a copy of, or delete your information, contact your clinic. Cavitex cannot access your records, so we cannot carry out these requests ourselves, but we will help any clinic that asks us how.

Clinics and staff

You can view, correct, export and delete records directly in the app. Depending on where you are, data-protection law may give you further rights over information we hold about you, such as your support emails. Contact us to use them; we reply within 30 days.

13.Children

Dentiva is a professional tool for dental clinics and is not intended for use by children. Clinics may record information about patients who are children; the clinic is responsible for getting any consent a parent or guardian must give.

14.International transfers

Cavitex does not transfer clinic or patient data anywhere. If you use Google Drive backup, Google may store the encrypted backups in data centres in other countries, under Google's own privacy policy.
