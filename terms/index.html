5.Records, backups and loss

Dentiva keeps records on your device. It is not a hosted service, and we hold no copy of your data. That means we cannot restore anything for you.

Export backups regularly and keep them somewhere safe. Uninstalling the app (on Windows, when you choose to remove its data as well), resetting it, or losing the device destroys the records on it. Local backup files are not encrypted, so store them as carefully as the records themselves.

A backup includes the clinic's photos, radiographs and prescription images unless they come to more than a backup can hold. The app tells you when that happens; until it no longer does, keeping a separate copy of the images is your responsibility.

6.Google Drive backup

Drive backup is optional and off until you turn it on. When you do, encrypted copies of your data are stored in your own Google account, under Google's terms. The encryption key stays on the device that made the backup, so a Drive backup can be restored on that device only.

You can disconnect at any time in Backup & Data, and remove Dentiva's access from your Google Account settings.
